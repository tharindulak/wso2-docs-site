## Question

Can WSO2 accept Algolia DocSearch's terms (effective 2026-07-24), including automatic enrolment in DocSearchMCP, which grants Algolia a sublicensable licence to use crawled docs for AI tools, the required "Search by Algolia" logo, and keystroke data sent to Algolia? And why did docs-agent-platform switch from Algolia to lunr on 2026-09-27? Context: [Search options](03-search-options.md).

## Checklist (for the human)

- [ ] Ask the docs-agent-platform team why Algolia was switched off on 2026-09-27.
- [ ] Send the DocSearch terms and the DocSearchMCP licence clause to WSO2 legal; record the verdict.
- [ ] Ask privacy/compliance whether search keystrokes going to Algolia need a consent banner entry or a DPA.
- [ ] Record outcome: approved, approved with conditions, or rejected (then Typesense is the path).
