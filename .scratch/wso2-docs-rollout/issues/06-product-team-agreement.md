## Question

Every product team agrees to publish docs releases with bundles through the shared Action and to switch on immutable releases; each team also agrees to its migration slot and cutover sign-off checklist. The agent drafts the per-team brief (what changes for writers, timeline, what the team does, what the docs-site team does); the human sends it and records each team's answer, owner and any conditions. Absorbs [Onboard product teams to docs releases](../../wso2-docs-site-versions/issues/12-onboard-teams-to-docs-releases.md). Contacting teams needs the user's go-ahead.
