# What "WSO2 Cloud" means to a customer

Parent: [map](../map.md)
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

Is "WSO2 Cloud" a shared customer account, subscription and console across the five products, or only an umbrella label? Today Integration Platform uses "WSO2 Cloud" as its own name and sign-up label, API Platform and Integration Platform appear to share a control plane, and Identity Platform bills separately. The answer fixes the CONTEXT.md definition and how the hub's "What is WSO2 Cloud" and sign-up pages are framed. Context: [Where cross-cutting topics live today](01-cross-cutting-topics-today.md), open questions 1 and 2.

## Answer

Resolved with the user, 2026-10-02.

- **WSO2 Cloud** is WSO2's managed SaaS family of five products; the **WSO2 Cloud account** is the single login and organization giving access to all of them (terms in CONTEXT.md).
- Today the products sign up separately. WSO2 has identified the gap; the single account is planned to ship before WSO2Con (2026-11-20). Internal, non-public date.
- Integration Platform's "WSO2 Cloud" wording is early use of the account brand, not a rename of the family. The hub calls the product "Integration Platform".
- Billing today: a shared WSO2 subscription covers API Platform, Integration Platform and Moesif Basic; Identity Platform bills separately. Effect of the single account on billing is unknown.
- Hub framing: honest family. One hub page, "WSO2 Cloud account and sign-up", describes the single account at launch, with a fallback version (per-product sign-up paths) ready if the account slips.
- Risk: the launch plan depends on an internal date. The hub publishes nothing about the single account until it ships or WSO2 approves the announcement.
- Bijira stays on the "Product names" page (user decision).
