# Add email OTP login

Email OTP sends a one-time password to the user's verified email address as a second authentication factor.

## Prerequisites

- An application registered in the WSO2 Identity Platform Console.
- A user account with a verified email address.

:::note[Before you begin]

Configure an email provider so that the OTP emails can be delivered. See [Configure the email provider](../../guides/notification-channels/configure-email-provider.md).

:::

## Step 1: Enable email OTP

1. On the Console, go to **Connections** and select **Email OTP**.
2. Set the OTP length and expiry time, and click **Update**.

<Foo kind="video" src="https://example.com/email-otp.mp4" />

## Step 2: Add email OTP to the login flow

1. Go to **Applications** and select your application.
2. In the **Login Flow** tab, click **+ Add Sign In Option** on the second step and select **Email OTP**.

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="visual" label="Visual Editor">

Drag **Email OTP** onto the second step.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="classic" label="Classic Editor">

Click **Add Authentication** and select **Email OTP**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Step 3: Try it out

Sign in to the application. After entering the password, you receive an email with a one-time password.

:::warning[Deprecated: legacy email OTP authenticator]

The legacy `EmailOTP` authenticator (configured in `deployment.toml`) is deprecated. Use the **Email OTP** connection instead.

:::
